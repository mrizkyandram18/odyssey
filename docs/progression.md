# Progression

## Overview

Odyssey tracks two parallel progression arcs:

1. **Individual** — an Explorer's growth, role mastery, and personal milestones.
2. **Family (Group)** — the family's collective advancement through the world.

Both arcs advance through the same actions. The dual-track design ensures that
every family member feels their contribution matters, while also reinforcing
that the group succeeds together.

## Individual Progression

### Explorer Levels

Each family member's **Explorer** gains **Experience (XP)** from:

- Completing daily turns.
- Finishing quest exercises.
- Contributing to creative spaces.
- Peer reactions to their contributions.

As XP accumulates, the Explorer **levels up** (Explorer Level). Each level
grants:

- A **Gift** containing a fixed Collection or cosmetic unlock.
- Periodic unlocks of new creative tools or narrative titles.

Leveling is intentionally smooth: the XP curve is gentle so that even casual,
infrequent play steadily advances the Explorer. There is no level cap in the
MVP; levels scale logarithmically so growth feels consistent over years.

### Role Mastery

Roles (Seeker, Builder, Guide) are narrative framing, not mechanical classes.
They do not grant stat bonuses, resource multipliers, or any mechanical advantage.
Each family member accrues **mastery** in the roles they exercise most.
Mastery is a quiet metric: it unlocks small narrative flavor (e.g., "Luna the
Seeker, Finder of Hidden Things") and occasional creative-tool shortcuts. It
never gates meaningful content or creates imbalance.

### Journey Progress

Progress is also tracked per **Journey**. As the family completes missions in a
journey, a shared Journey Progress bar fills. Reaching thresholds unlocks:

- New missions in the journey.
- A **Journey Gift** (shared, with crew-wide cosmetics).
- Narrative concept.

### Personal Milestones

Achievements tied to a single family member's journey:

- **First Mission** — complete your first quest.
- **Collector** — gather N story fragments.
- **Builder** — contribute N creative-space items.
- **Streak Keeper** — maintain a daily streak for N days.
- **Storyteller** — contribute N story-branch sentences.

Milestones award XP, a Collection, and a small narrative title.

## Family (Group) Progression

### Family Level

The family's **crew level** advances from the sum of all members' quest
completions and story milestones. It is displayed prominently on the home screen
as a shared progress bar. Family level unlocks:

- New **realms** in the world.
- Shared **creative-space tools** (available to all members).
- **Group quest lines** that require coordination.

### Journey Unlocks

The world is gated by story and crew progress. Each journey is unlocked through
a mix of:

- Completing the preceding journey's story arc.
- Reaching a crew level threshold.
- Reaching a Journey Progress threshold in the previous journey.

### Group Milestones

Shared accomplishments:

- **All members visited a journey.**
- **Collective quest count** (e.g., 50 missions completed as a family).
- **Family streak** — every family member completes a daily turn in the same week.
- **Harmony badges** — earned for balanced participation (everyone contributed
  in a given week).

Group milestones unlock crew-wide cosmetics (a shared banner, a journey theme)
and narrative concept.

## Progression Rhythm

The rhythm is designed for a family that plays together a few times a week:

| Cadence | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | One daily turn unlocks; streak tracking. |
| **Weekly** | A shared group milestone check; a mini story-event. |
| **Monthly** | A new quest line or creative-space feature unlocks. |
| **Seasonal** | A new journey theme and narrative arc (≈ every 3 months). |

## Replayability

- **Branch choices:** Different story branches in missions create mild replay
  incentive. Branches are low-cost to re-explore.
- **Journey Progress replay:** Returning to completed realms reveals new
  dialogue and hidden story fragments.
- **Role rotation:** Encouraging each member to try different roles surfaces
  different quest content and story commentary.

## Anti-FOMO & Anti-Grind

- No progression is locked behind daily logins alone. Regular play leads to
  faster progression, but sporadic play is always viable.
- There are no limited-time-exclusive items. Seasonal content rotates back.
- Family progression is never blocked by a single inactive member.
