# Vision

## One-Liner

Odyssey is a cooperative adventure platform where a private family group works
together to explore a shared world, complete quests, and grow together — purely
for fun, learning, and engagement.

## The Big Picture

Odyssey is **not** another reward application. It is a lightweight, persistent,
cooperative adventure game designed for a small, closed family circle of roughly
eight users. Every mechanic is built around three questions:

1. Does it bring the family together?
2. Does it invite curiosity, creativity, or learning?
3. Does it feel like play?

Real-world rewards — if they ever happen at all — live exclusively in the
**Family Reward** system. Inside Odyssey there is **no money, no gambling, no
pay-to-win**. There is only game progression, shared discovery, and the
satisfaction of completing something as a group.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** A single extended family (≈ 8 members), ranging from adults to
  children old enough to read with assistance.
- **Secondary:** Future family groups may be added, but each instance is a
  separate, invite-only circle. The product never aims for public scale.

## What Success Looks Like

- Family members log in regularly, not because they *must*, but because they
  *want* to see what happens next.
- Quests are completed collaboratively — a younger member might do research,
  a parent might verify a real-world step, and together they advance the story.
- The family develops inside jokes, nicknames, and shared milestones.
- Parents report that Odyssey sparked a real-world conversation, a learning
  moment, or a creative project outside the app.

## What This Project Is

| Aspect | Decision |
|---|---|
| Experience | A persistent, story-driven cooperative adventure |
| Core mechanic | Shared quests, collaborative challenges, group progression |
| Identity model | Each family member has an Explorer with a role; the family has a shared crew |
| Progression | Experience, skills, achievements, and story chapters |
| Economy | Soft resources only (no currency exchange, no real money) |
| Platform | Web PWA, mobile-first, offline-capable core loop |
| Authentication | Gatekeeper (BOTH login mode) — the single identity source |

## Out of Scope

See [Non-Goals](non-goals.md) for the full list. In summary: no gambling, no
employee management, no MMORPG-scale content, no microtransactions, and no
feature creep beyond the cooperative adventure core.

## Guiding Metaphor

Think of Odyssey as a **shared storybook that writes itself**. Each family
member's actions fill in the next page, and every page is visible to everyone.
The system's job is to make each turn feel meaningful, surprising, and
inherently rewarding — without relying on external incentives.
